Bees Keys for schools and districts

Bees Keys is designed to work smoothly on managed devices. Already using it at your school? We'd love to hear how, and what would make it even more useful.

How it works at school

  • Managed distribution Bees Keys is available through Apple School Manager and Apps and Books. Assign it to devices with your MDM like any other app. Bees Keys on the App Store (App Store ID 1608004053).
  • No accounts, no student data There is no sign-in and nothing to enroll. The app collects no personal information, shows no ads, and runs no analytics or third-party tracking. Read the privacy policy. It's short.
  • Nothing to configure Once installed it works offline, and there is no per-student setup. Open it and play.
  • Any pedagogical method Solfège or letter names, naturals only or full chromatic, slow or fast. Set it to the method you already teach rather than the other way round.
  • Free printables for the classroom Worksheets and games to go with the app, free to print as many copies as you need. See the free resources →

Bees Keys Pro at school

Pro adds Live play: put the iPad on the music desk and the app hears the student answer on a real piano, through the microphone or over MIDI. Interested in Pro for a music room, or for a whole school? Tell us. We're working out the best way to offer it to institutions.
