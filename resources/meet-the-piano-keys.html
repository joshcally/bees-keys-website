Free Piano Key Name Worksheets for Beginner Students

Published August 11, 2026

One of my piano students doesn't use technology at home.

Since so much of my beginner teaching is reinforced through Bees Keys, I still wanted her to have a way to practice those same piano key-name skills between lessons. So I made her some worksheets!

They worked so well that I decided to turn them into a full printable set to share with other piano teachers.

These worksheets are designed as a simple introduction to the pattern of the piano keyboard and the names of the white keys. Students practice recognizing groups of two and three black keys, finding individual notes, and getting comfortable with keyboard geography.
