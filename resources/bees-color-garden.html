Bee's Color Garden: Free Color-by-Key Piano Worksheets

Published August 16, 2026

Click to download the free PDF

Color every flower to match its piano key.

The garden is full of flowers, and every flower carries a key name. Along the bottom, keyboard diagrams pair one highlighted key with one color. Students read each highlighted key from the black-key groups — the way they will at the piano — then hunt down its flowers and color them to match. One garden covers C, D, and E; the other covers F, G, A, and B.

Then play it on Bees Keys! 🐝

The worksheet asks your student to find every C, D, or E in the garden. The app asks the same question at the keyboard — a bee flies in carrying a note name, and they tap the matching key before it escapes. Same skill, fresh combinations every time, with the answer confirmed the instant they tap.

Worksheet, then piano, then app. Practicing all three solidifies your student's understanding of the keyboard pattern.
