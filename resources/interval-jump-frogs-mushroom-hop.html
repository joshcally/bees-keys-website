Frog's Mushroom Hop

A Color-by-Interval Coloring Sheet

By Kristianna Callahan · Published October 3, 2026

Click to download the free PDF

A cute coloring sheet for early interval recognition at the piano! Choose your own color for each interval, then color the mushrooms to match.

There are two versions included: one with repeat, step, and skip, and another with unison, 2nd, and 3rd. Use the worksheets separately, or use them together to help students connect the two different ways of naming the same intervals.

Super simple, and a fun little way to practice recognizing intervals on the staff!

From the page to the river 🐸

These are the same repeats, steps, and skips students practice in Interval Jump! In the app, Frog jumps from log to log while students quickly identify each interval on the staff.

The worksheet gives students a little more time to look and think, then the game lets them practice recognizing the same patterns a little faster.
