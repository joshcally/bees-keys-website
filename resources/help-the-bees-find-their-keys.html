Help the Bees Find Their Keys: Free Piano Key Names Worksheet

Connect each bee to the correct piano key.

Seven bees are carrying the letters A through G, and every one of them needs to land on the right key. Students draw a line from each bee down to its key, using the groups of 2 and 3 black keys to find their way, exactly the way they will at the piano.
