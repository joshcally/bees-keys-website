Where the bugs come from 🐸

In Interval Jump, a frog crosses a river by leaping between notes on the staff. Once a run, a lily pad drifts into the gap ahead with a bug waiting on it: hop out and back to the next log before the pad sinks, and don't splash, or the bug is gone. The bug you catch escorts the frog to the island.

Catch it in the app, then stick it on the card. Or skip the app entirely and hand out a firefly for a well-practiced scale. The stickers don't mind.
