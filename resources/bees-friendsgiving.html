Bee’s Friendsgiving

A Piano Key-Name Puzzle

By Kristianna Callahan · Published November 2, 2026

Click to download the free PDF

Bee is hosting Friendsgiving, and six bug friends are on the way! Each guest’s name is missing a few letters. The piano keys fill them in: every blank has a small keyboard above it showing one key inside a 2 or 3 black-key group. Name the key, write the letter, and the guest list spells itself out.

It’s a fun way to practice finding C, D and E inside the group of 2 black keys and F, G, A and B inside the group of 3, with a little spelling on the side.

A few ideas for how to use this puzzle:

  1. Solve it: Name each key, write the letter in the blank, and read the guest’s name out loud.
  2. Play the guests: Once a name is filled in, play every piano-letter in it on the keyboard, in order.
  3. Find the group first: Before writing, have the student say whether each key lives in the group of 2 or the group of 3 black keys.
  4. Laminate the color copy: Dry-erase markers wipe clean, so one sheet lasts the whole season.

Then play it on Bees Keys! 🐝

The puzzle asks your student to name a key inside the black-key groups. The app asks the same question at the keyboard: a bee flies in carrying a note name, and they tap the matching key before it escapes. Same skill, fresh combinations every time, with the answer confirmed the instant they tap.
