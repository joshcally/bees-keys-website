Bee’s Friendsgiving

A Free Piano Key Worksheet

By Kristianna Callahan · Published November 2, 2026

Click to download the free PDF

Bee is hosting Friendsgiving, and needs help remembering who is on the invite list.

This week’s free printable is a piano key puzzle. Students will use the highlighted piano keys to find the missing letters and figure out which bugs are coming to Friendsgiving.

This activity is great for beginners who are learning to recognize the piano keys by their groups of two and three black keys.

I’ve also included a non-holiday version called Bee’s Friends, so you can use this worksheet any time of year.
