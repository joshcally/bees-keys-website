Which Key Is Mine? Free Key-Naming Piano Worksheets

Published August 17, 2026

Click to download the free PDF

Every bee knows its letter. Can your student find its key?

Eight bees, each hovering over its own little slice of the keyboard, each carrying a letter name. Students read the letter, find that key from the black-key groups — the way they will at the real piano — and color it in. One worksheet covers C, D, and E around the group of 2; the other covers F, G, A, and B around the group of 3.

Two worksheets — C-D-E and F-G-A-B — in both versions.

Great for using during a lesson and for sending home as homework.

Then play it on Bees Keys! 🐝

The worksheet asks your student to match each bee's letter to its key. The app asks the same question at the keyboard — a bee flies in carrying a note name, and they tap the matching key before it escapes. Same skill, fresh combinations every time, with the answer confirmed the instant they tap.

Worksheet, then piano, then app. Practicing all three solidifies your student's understanding of the keyboard pattern.
