Now play it on your real piano.

Bees Keys listens. Put the iPad on the music desk, and the bee's note is answered on the actual instrument, not a picture of one. The moment a student connects the screen to the keys under their hands is the moment the lesson lands.

  • Any piano Acoustic, digital, or grand. It hears you through the microphone.
  • Or plug in Connect a digital piano over MIDI or Bluetooth for exact, instant answers.
