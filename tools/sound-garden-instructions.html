Bees Keys

Bee's Sound Garden

Eighteen little stories from the garden. Draw a card, then play it on the piano. There are no wrong notes.

    Materials needed:
  • Piano
  • Cards
  • Scissors
  1. 1 Cut out the cards and shuffle them. Place the deck face down on the music stand.
  2. 2 Draw a card. Read the title, then the little line underneath. That line is the shape of your sound.
  3. 3 Play the story. Use any keys you like, as many as you like, for as long as you like. The only thing to follow is the shape: if the card says HIGH, play up at the thin end of the piano; if it says LOW, play down at the fat end.
  4. 4 Play it again, differently. Louder, softer, faster, slower, with one finger or a whole handful. Same card, brand new adventure.

The one rule: there are no wrong notes in the garden. If it sounds like the story, it is right.

More ways to play

  • Guess my card The teacher draws a card and plays it. The student guesses which creature it was, then swaps places.
  • Play it together On the two-voice cards, one player takes the HIGH voice and the other takes the LOW voice at the same time.
  • Draw your own The second deck leaves the picture blank. Students color in their own garden, then play what they drew.
