Bee's Sound Garden

Explore improvisation right at the beginning!

Bee's Sound Garden is a way for students to imagine what different sounds in the real world could sound like at the piano. Choose pre-illustrated cards, illustrate your own cards, or let your students have fun creating sounds and drawings to match.

    Materials needed:
  • Piano
  • Cards
  • Scissors

Ways to play

  1. 1 Print your cards, leave it as a sheet or cut them out and pick at random.
  2. 2 Tell a story! Let your imagination take you through one card, or a series of cards.
  3. 3 Draw your own story, then play it at the piano.
  4. 4 Pay attention to the highs/lows on the card!
  5. 5 For older students, let them illustrate their own card, choose a scale they’re working on, and improvise using that scale over a simple I–IV–V–I progression. They can play the chords in their left hand, or you can play the progression as a duet.
  6. 6 Guess my card! The teacher draws a card and plays it. The student guesses which creature it was, then swap places.
  7. 7 Play it together. On the two-voice cards, one player takes the HIGH voice and the other takes the LOW voice at the same time.

Two decks in one download

Use the pre-illustrated cards, or come up with your own illustrations on the blank cards. Drawing the card first, then playing it is often the part that unlocks a shy improviser.
