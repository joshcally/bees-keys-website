Bees Keys

Find Every Key

1-2 players

You'll need: the cards · a piano · a stopwatch (a phone timer works) · a dry-erase marker for the score board

  1. 1 Shuffle the cards and place the deck face down on the music stand.
  2. 2 Flip the top card. Find every one of that key on the piano — play them all, from the lowest to the highest!
  3. 3 Too easy? Time it. Write your best time on the board on the back of this sheet, then try to beat it next lesson.
  4. 4 Special cards: the Group cards mean find every group of 2 or 3 black keys. Middle C, the Lowest Key, and the Highest Key have just one right answer — find it fast! Your Choice means the player picks any key to hunt.
Playing with two? Take turns on the same card and time each player — the faster time keeps the card. Most cards at the end of the deck wins!

Bees Keys Pro times every round for you. Find Every is part of the Pro upgrade, and it's played on your real piano — the app listens by microphone or MIDI and keeps every best time automatically.
