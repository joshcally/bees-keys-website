Free resources for piano teachers

Printables made in a real studio, for real beginners. Everything here is free to download, free to print, and free to use with your students — no sign-up, no email, no watermark.

  • Free printable

    Bug Collection Sticker Sheets

    • 756 stickers
    • 12-page PDF
    • US Letter

    Seven sheets of bug stickers and three kinds of collection card to cut out — a 6-slot card for a week of lessons, a 9-slot card for a month, and Collect All Six, which shows the bug each slot is waiting for. Reward stickers for any lesson, on any instrument.

    Get the free printable →

  • Free printable

    Which Key Is Mine?

    • 4 worksheets
    • 6-page PDF
    • US Letter

    Eight bees, each carrying a letter above its own little slice of the keyboard. Students read the letter, find its key from the black-key groups, and color it in. Covers C-D-E and F-G-A-B — printer-friendly plus colorful versions to laminate and reuse.

    Get the free printable →

  • Free printable

    Bee's Color Garden

    • 4 worksheets
    • 6-page PDF
    • US Letter

    A garden of lettered flowers, and keyboard diagrams that pair each highlighted key with a color. Students name the key, then color every matching flower. Two gardens — C-D-E and F-G-A-B — each with a choose-your-own-colors version.

    Get the free printable →

  • Free printable

    Help the Bees Find Their Keys

    • 1 worksheet
    • 4-page PDF
    • US Letter

    Seven bees, each carrying a letter from A to G, and a keyboard waiting underneath. Students draw a line from every bee to its key — using the black-key groups to find their way. Printer-friendly plus a colorful version to laminate and reuse.

    Get the free printable →

  • Free printable

    Groups of Black Keys

    • 4 worksheets
    • 6-page PDF
    • US Letter

    The first piano lesson: keyboard geography. Students trace the number, count the keys, and circle every group of 2 and 3 black keys they can find. Printer-friendly pages plus colorful ones to laminate and reuse.

    Get the free printable →

    Meet the Piano Keys

    • US Letter

    Trace, write, find, and color your way through the white keys A to G. Two versions in one download: printer-friendly pages for crayons and pencils, and colorful pages to laminate and reuse with a dry-erase marker.
