Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

BeesKeys is built to teach piano, not to collect data. This policy explains the little there is to explain.

Personal Information

BeesKeys does not require an account or sign-in. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no analytics services.

Microphone

If you allow microphone access, BeesKeys listens only to detect which piano notes you play, and only while the app is in use. Audio is processed entirely on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

MIDI pianos

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, note information is used only to operate the game, and remains on your device.

Purchases

BeesKeys Pro is a one-time purchase processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

App Data

Your app preferences, game settings, and locally stored game data (such as best game times) are saved only on your device. This information is not transmitted to us.

Children

BeesKeys is designed to be safe for learners of all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at beeskeysapp@gmail.com.

If anything changes, we'll update this policy here.
