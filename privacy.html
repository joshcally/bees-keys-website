Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Bees Keys and Interval Jump are built to teach music, not to collect data. This policy explains the little there is to explain.

Apps Covered by This Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Bees Keys and Interval Jump apps distributed through Apple's App Store and the Google Play Store.

Personal Information

Neither app requires an account or sign-in. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. The apps contain no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no analytics services.

Microphone

If you allow microphone access in Bees Keys, the app listens only to detect which piano notes you play, and only while the app is in use. Audio is processed entirely on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

MIDI pianos

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, note information is used only to operate the game, and remains on your device.

Purchases

Any purchases made through our apps are processed securely by the store where you downloaded the app: Apple through the App Store or Google through the Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

App Data

Your app preferences, game settings, and locally stored game data (such as best game times) are saved only on your device. This information is not transmitted to us.

Children

Bees Keys and Interval Jump are designed to be safe for learners of all ages. Because the apps do not collect personal information, they do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at beeskeysapp@gmail.com.

If anything changes, we'll update this policy here.
